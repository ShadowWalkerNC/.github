### Key Systemic Distinctions

#### 1. Registry Validation $\neq$ Authorization Grant
* **Metadata Hygiene Only:** `validateRegistry()` inspects envelope versioning, ID format constraints, enum validity, and relative path traversal safety (`path.resolve` + `fs.realpathSync`).
* **Non-Authoritative:** A passing validation check records catalog completeness. It is not an authorization grant, cryptographic signature verification, or role assignment. State transitions to `ADOPTED` cannot bypass runtime execution barriers or administrative approvals.

#### 2. `simulationPASS` vs `realacceptanceNOTRUN`
* **`simulationPASS` Scope:** Verifies internal control-flow branches, argument validation (`INVALID_REQUEST`, `INVALID_PARAMS`), not-found signaling, and error formatting against synthetic in-memory fixtures.
* **`realacceptanceNOTRUN` Scope:** No physical ESP32 or serial/TCP connection is engaged, no discovery broadcast or packet framing is verified, and no hardware-level timing, security boundary, or transport handshake exists. Simulated success must never be cited as real protocol acceptance.
