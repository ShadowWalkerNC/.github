# ADR-0003 — Muse-first R&D through MuseLab
Status: accepted strategic intent; implementation pending
Owner: ShadowWalkerNC
Date: 2026-10-03

## Requirement and context
Strategic experimentation needs an incubation home without production dependency sprawl.

## Decision
Use MuseLab for Muse-first experiments with hypothesis, implementation, results and adoption decision. Simulate devices before buying hardware.

## Alternatives and consequences
Other providers may be compared for a concrete requirement. Production promotion needs evidence and explicit approval; MuseAiBots has not been renamed.
