# Historical archive

Owner: ShadowWalkerNC · Version: 1.0.0 · Applies to: historical records only.

The 2026-11-01 snapshot preserves previous guidance, blueprints, decisions, phase
records, and claims for interpretation, not execution. Its contents are **not
current instructions or proof of present product behavior**. Original bytes remain
in Git at the base commit recorded in [index.json](index.json).

Snapshots are sanitized: machine paths and nonpublic identities are redacted.
Historical relative links may refer to absent external evidence. They are not
active acceptance gates. Do not execute archived scripts. No history was rewritten.
No historical product-specific strategic decision is revoked by archiving it;
confirm its applicability against the named product's current owner and docs.
New strategic decisions belong in that product unless they change this library.

The migration map in index.json preserves old entry points. Active navigation
starts at the repository README; product state belongs with product evidence.

Private identity-bearing historical paths are intentionally not republished.
The archive map uses opaque paths plus original-path SHA-256 values for those
entries; originals remain recoverable at the recorded base Git commit.
