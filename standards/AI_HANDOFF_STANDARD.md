# Shared AI handoff standard
Start from current phase, shared ledger, portfolio registry, target manifest/status/instructions and relevant files only.
Before simultaneous work, record actor, task ID, paths, expected artifact and ownership. Avoid conflicting edits. Listed actors are not permission to dispatch another agent.
Record material changed files, reason, checks actually run, build/deploy status, issues, durable decisions and next action.
Never record private reasoning or secrets. Keep provider observations timestamped and unresolved facts explicit.
Release claims at turn end. Preserve other agents' research and source changes. Multi-agent execution requires owner approval.
