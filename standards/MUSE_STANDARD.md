# Muse Standard

Owner: ShadowWalkerNC · Version: 1.0.0 · Applies to: explicitly adopted project scope.
Canonical location: `standards/MUSE_STANDARD.md`.

MuseLab is Muse-first R&D, not a second production platform. Every experiment records hypothesis, implementation, results and decision; benchmarks are required when claiming improvement. Separate Muse Code, model APIs, connectors, gadgets, voice/media and XR surfaces. Simulate devices first. Evidence and explicit approval precede production promotion.
