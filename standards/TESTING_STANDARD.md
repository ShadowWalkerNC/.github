# testing standard
Match checks to changed risk: targeted units, affected integration tests, versioned contract tests and end-to-end flows as relevant. Do not weaken tests to obtain a pass. Preserve PASS/FAIL/NOT RUN/BLOCKED distinctions and artifact evidence. Source inspection and local builds do not certify production, clinical, payment, hardware or visual behavior.
